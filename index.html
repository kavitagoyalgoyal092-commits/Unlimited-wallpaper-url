Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Welcome to Unlimited Wallpaper. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information when you use our wallpaper services.

Privacy Summary: Unlimited Wallpaper is designed to provide wallpaper browsing, downloading and wallpaper-setting functionality. We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the core wallpaper features.

1. Information We Collect

We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, home address, passwords or account credentials through the core functionality of the application.

The application may process technical information required for network requests, content loading, caching and application functionality.

2. Internet Access

Unlimited Wallpaper requires Internet access to retrieve wallpaper images and videos from third-party content services.

If you are offline, previously downloaded or cached wallpapers may remain available depending on the application's local storage and cache.

3. Wallpaper Images and Videos

Wallpaper images and videos may be retrieved from third-party content providers, including Pixabay.

The application may request publicly available wallpaper content through these services. The availability and licensing of third-party content is controlled by the respective content provider.

4. Downloads and Local Storage

When you choose to download a wallpaper, the selected image or video may be stored locally on your device.

These downloaded files are controlled by you and can be removed from your device using the normal device file-management functionality.

5. Dynamic / Live Wallpapers

If you choose to use a video as a dynamic or live wallpaper, the application may store the selected video locally so that Android can use it as a live wallpaper.

The application does not need to upload your selected wallpaper back to our servers for this functionality.

6. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services to provide wallpaper content and technical functionality.

These services may process requests according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the relevant third-party providers for more information about how they handle requests and technical information.

7. Advertising

If advertisements are displayed in the application, advertising providers may collect certain information according to their own policies, such as advertising identifiers, device information and interaction data.

Advertising providers may use this information to provide, measure and improve advertisements.

8. Analytics

If analytics services are integrated into the application, those services may collect technical and usage information according to their respective privacy policies.

If no analytics service is integrated, the application does not use analytics for the purposes described above.

9. Permissions

The application may request permissions that are necessary for specific functionality, such as Internet access or saving downloaded wallpapers.

Permissions are used only for their intended application functionality.

10. Children's Privacy

Unlimited Wallpaper is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the core functionality of the application.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Retention

Since the core application does not require users to create accounts, we do not maintain a personal user account database for the core wallpaper functionality.

Downloaded wallpapers and cached content may remain on your device until you remove them or the application clears them.

13. Your Choices

You can control the application's access to certain device permissions through Android's system settings.

You can also remove downloaded wallpaper files and uninstall the application at any time.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, services or applicable requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support/contact method provided in the application or its store listing.

16. Acceptance

By using Unlimited Wallpaper, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
